    Home support

    Your regions

    Developing a region raises its income for good. Regions pay only while you hold them, so a developed region is also a juicy target.

    Nations at peace ignore each other's troops. Declaring war costs home support and makes everyone trust you less. Allies may be asked to join your wars.

    Strategy
    Select units
    Squads

    Drag on the map to box-select, then tap the ground to send them. Tap an enemy to focus fire. Double-tap a unit to grab all of its type on screen. Keys: 1–6 pick a squad, Ctrl+1–6 saves one, A/M/P set the tap order, H hold, T take cover, R fall back.

    Construct (engineers build it)

    Pick a structure, then tap the map near your base. Your selected engineers go and build it, or the nearest free ones if none are selected.

    Support costs supplies and needs time to recharge. Some need a radar, an airfield or a missile silo. Pick one, then tap the map.

    Strength estimates come only from what your units have seen. Old sightings fade, and armies you have never scouted are a guess.
